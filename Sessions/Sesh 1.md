# Prep:
[[Daimon]] wakes up in a lab, surrounded by cultzists, he has to escape.
He manages, and runs out.
He tries to navigate the dungeon, and eventually stumbles upon [[Bob Bobber]] & [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]].

[[Desmodus (WAR-01)]] is broken and rusty and will need an artificer to fix him. 
(his arm has popped off and his weapon is malfunctioning) 
(if he tries using his malfunctioning weapon, have him roll a Con Saving Throw:)

| roll  | Consequenses                        |
| ----- | ----------------------------------- |
| 1-5   | take 1d4 damage                     |
| 6-8   | Weapon-lock until fixed             |
| 9-11  | Missed Attack                       |
| 12-15 | it works normally                   |
| 16-20 | +1d4 dmg                            |
| 20+   | +1d4 dmg & Advantage on next action |

They fight their way through the dungeon and go outside.
(throw some Cultists and Cultist Fanatics at them. if they're doing good, MAYBE throw a Fiend  Cultist at them, but not the Hierophant.)

They find their way to [[Ascot]] where [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]] is feared, [[Bob Bobber]] is looked at weirdly and [[Daimon]] has to hide his body, because the spells on his body are forbidden.
