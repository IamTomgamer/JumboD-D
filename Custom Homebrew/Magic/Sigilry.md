**Sigilry** is a magic system, originating from [[Arborea]], which uses [[Conjuring Ink]] to use magic. spells can be created by combining a seal (a ring), a Sigil (the center icon) and Signs.

Example:
Wallbreaker Spell; creates a circular hole in a wall by combinging an Earth Sigil, 2 collumn signs (sides) and two crushing signs (up and down).
Note that this spell would not activate, due to the Seal not being closed
![[Wallbreaker Spell.png]]
Learn more about Signs [here](https://witchhatatelier.telepedia.net/wiki/Signs_Explained)
Learn more about Sigils [here](https://witchhatatelier.telepedia.net/wiki/Sigils_Explained)

The items needed for this magic system are:
* [[Conjuring Ink]]
* a simple Ink Pen
* [[Palm Quire]]
* [[Woodcruor wand]]

The Calculation for the rolling of a Sigilry spell is:
every unique sign/sigil * (1+(0.5 * amount of seal rings)) and round down.
roll with just enough d20s.
