the **Woodcruor wand** is a wand primarily made of dead Silverwood, which can draw spells on hard surfaces like stone.
It has 500 charges.

1 charge is used when something is drawn the size of a palm quire
2 charges are used when something is drawn the size of a seat
3 charges are used when something is drawn the size of a Bike
4 charges are used when something is drawn the size of a car
