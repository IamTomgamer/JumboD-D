a **Palm Quire** can store up to 500 pieces of round paper, the size of a palm.
These pieces of paper can store 1 small spell and these can be drawn before use, but can only be used once.

A long rest can be used to prepare spells, and new pieces of paper can be bought in a Sigilist's shop
