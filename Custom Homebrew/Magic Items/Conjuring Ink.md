**Conjuring ink*** is ink used for a drawn magic system, known as [[Sigilry]].
**Conjuring ink** is made from the sticks of a dead Silverwood Tree and water.
Other ingredients can be added to alter the ink, such as:
* [[Azuremoon Flowers]], extends the duration of a spell
* [[Sphinx of wonder's strand of hair]], causes the ink to glow in the dark
* [[Golden Wyrm Scales]], turns spell ink invisible
* [[Quasit scales]], makes the ink waterproof
* [[Gargoyle Wings]], makes the ink turn to magic stone.
* [[Dwarven Blood]], makes the magic significantly more powerful, uncontrollable and highly unpredictable. using this Blood in ink is also highly illegal.
