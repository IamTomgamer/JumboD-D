Drawn on a big stone floor, **Serpent's Bed of Sand** creates a big, Cloud-like pillow that uses crushed stone (Sand) for stability and repetition to make the cloud keep its form.

When rolling to draw this spell, this spell needs 2d20, and will only succeed with an 31 or higher.
