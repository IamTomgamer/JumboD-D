# Session 1:
[[Daimon]] escapes from his [[The Brimhats|cult]]'s dungeon and finds [[Bob Bobber]] & [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]].
[[Desmodus (WAR-01)]] is broken and rusty and will need an artificer to fix him. 
(his arm has popped off and his weapon is malfunctioning) 
(if he tries using his malfunctioning weapon, have him roll a Con Saving Throw. 1-5 = take 1d4 force damage, 6-8 = Weapon-lock until fixed, 9-11 = missed attack, 12-15 = it works, 16-20 = +1d4 attack damage, 20+ = +1d4 attack damage & Advantage on next action)

They fight their way through the dungeon and go outside.
(throw some Cultists and Cultist Fanatics at them. if they're doing good, MAYBE throw a Fiend  Cultist at them, but not the Hierophant.)

They find their way to a nearby town where [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]] is feared, [[Bob Bobber]] is looked at weirdly and [[Daimon]] has to hide his body, because the spells on his body are forbidden.

There are a couple of shops and businesses.
- [[Tavern "The Wandering Basilisk"]]
- a library
- an investigator
- a Farmer's Shop
- an Artificer's Shop
- a Blacksmith
