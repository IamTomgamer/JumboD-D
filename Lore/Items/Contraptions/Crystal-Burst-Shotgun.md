the **Crystal-Shotgun** is a [[Sigilry]] [[Contraption]] that creates crystal Orbs and launches them at a high speed.
![[CrystalOrbShotgun.png|286]]
This shotgun uses a stamping method to reapply ink perfectly, and a twisting mechanism to activate the spell.
Any [[Conjuring Ink]] vial can be attached, including a [[twinned bottle]]
