# The Shadow
There's a ship called "The Shadow", which is believed to take control of any crew that tries boarding it, hanging the crew and blinding the captain before binding his hands to the helm.
When a captain tries to take her helm, her rigging comes alive, she hangs the crew, steals the sight of the captain, cursing him the Captain of the Shadow
## Quotes
"None can tame the one the sailors call the shadow"
