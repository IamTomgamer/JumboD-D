# The fourth and last type.
[[Raldrak]], [[Mustril]] & [[Lornebyrn]] created the last & controversial [[Warforged (X-series)|X-series of warforged]], a whopping 99 years after the inception of the [[Warforged (F-series)|F-series of warforged]].
This series of Warforged was made to kill and overpower their opponents in every way by combining everything from earlier models and adding more.
the X-series has 6 arms;
- 2 Cannons
- 2 [[Crystal Shotgun|shotguns]]
- 2 hands
The X-series also has:
- Wings
- [[Sylph Shoes]]
- Incredible strength & Absorption in all its bodyparts
- Knowledge on all Wizard spells
- Knowledge on Sigilist magic

only 5 were made of these Warforged.
