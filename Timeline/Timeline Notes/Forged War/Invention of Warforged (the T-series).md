# The second type.
[[Raldrak]], [[Thurgeal]] & [[Lornebyrn]] created the [[Warforged (T-series)|T-series of warforged]], roughly 35 years after the inception of what was later dubbed the [[Warforged (WAR-series)|WAR-series of warforged]].
This series was created to be less versatile, but more durable, bigger, heavier and stronger.
The T-series have magic cannons for hands.
Roughly 750 were made of these warforged.
