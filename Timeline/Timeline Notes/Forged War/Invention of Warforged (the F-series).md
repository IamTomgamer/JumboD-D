# The third type.
[[Raldrak]], [[Thurgeal]] & [[Lornebyrn]] created the [[Warforged (F-series)|F-series of warforged]], only 5 years after the inception of the [[Warforged (T-series)|T-series of warforged]].
This series of Warforged was made to Fly, using these Mechanisms.
1. [[Lornebyrn]] gave the F-series [[Sylph Shoes|sylph]] spells under their feet to shoot up into the air
2. [[Raldrak]] gave the F-series wings to soar through the air.
3. [[Thurgeal]] gave the F-series strong legs to absorb any landing force.

1005 were made of these Warforged
