# The [[Warforged (X-series)|X-series]] killing-spree
The [[Warforged (X-series)|X-series]] started killing all humans until they had to go into hiding. When the x-series found no more humans or dwarves, they turned on their creators and started killing dwarves.
Luckily, [[Lornebyrn]] had put a self-destruct sequence into the X-series, which is activated by twisting and pressing a knob in their neck.

A group of earlier series Warforged was tasked with doing this.
This group consisted of:
- [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]] (Living)
- [[WAR-02]]
- [[T-09]]
- [[T-16]]
- [[F-02]] (Living)
- [[F-13]]
Only [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]] and [[F-02]] survived, with a wiped memory. and one last model of the [[Warforged (X-series)|X-series]] running free, [[X-05]]
