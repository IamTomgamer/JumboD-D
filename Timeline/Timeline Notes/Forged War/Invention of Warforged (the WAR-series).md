# Warforged were invented by the Dwarves.
![[WAR-01.png]]
The first 20 years of the war were filled with Death of millions of dwarves.
Eventually, 3 dwarves started working on a project, the result of which created the [[Warforged (WAR-series)|WAR-series]], the first of which being [[Desmodus (WAR-01)]].
These dwarves were [[Raldrak]], [[Thurgeal]] and [[Lornebyrn]].


Roughly 2000 were made of these warforged.

After creating the WAR-series, these three dwarves created more series for more specific purposes.
